Ember Courier

Ember Courier is Jovaryn Games' first original playable game. Explore a woodland environment, carry an ember, collect sparks and deliver its light to beacons.

The project represents our next step into original game development. It is a playable prototype, not a finished commercial product.

ABOUT JOVARYN GAMES

Building Original Interactive Experiences.

Jovaryn Games began with browser games and Minecraft Bedrock add-ons. These projects helped us develop skills in programming, game design, gameplay systems, testing and project organization.

We are now developing original games, beginning with Ember Courier, while preserving our earlier projects as part of our development portfolio.

Our focus is on creating enjoyable experiences through continuous learning, careful development and honest progress.
